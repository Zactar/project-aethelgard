# Frogger River Crossing

A classic 2D top-down arcade action game where the player navigates a frog avatar across a hazardous multi-lane highway and a rapid river to reach safety bays.

## Core Gameplay
- The player moves in 4 cardinal directions on a discrete 2D grid.
- Lane 1-3: Traffic Highway. Trucks, cars, and bulldozers moving horizontally at varying speeds. Touching a vehicle kills the player.
- Lane 4: Median safety pavement.
- Lane 5-7: River Torrent. Turtles and logs floating horizontally. The player must jump on logs/turtles to cross. Stepping into water kills the player.
- Goal: Fill all 5 home bays at the top of the screen before the countdown timer expires.

## Scoring & Rules
- 10 points for each forward hop.
- 50 points for filling a home bay.
- 3 Lives. Losing all lives triggers Game Over.
